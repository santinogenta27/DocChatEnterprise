“So What the Heck Do I Do Now?”  
So what now? what do you do? where do you start?

“Talk is cheap.” I hope you enjoyed reading this book, but
more important, I hope you use its principles to dramatically
enhance your life. In my experience, however, reading alone
will not make the difference you are looking for. Reading is a
start, but if you want to succeed in the real world, it’s going to
be your actions that count.

money blueprint. It’s simple: your financial blueprint will
determine your financial destiny. Make certain you do each of
the exercises I suggested in the arenas of verbal programming,
modeling, and specific incidents, in order to begin changing
your blueprint to one that supports financial success. I also
encourage you to do the declarations I suggested each and
every day.
